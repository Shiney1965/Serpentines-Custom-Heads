# Serpentine's Custom Heads

**TL/DR:** Sixteen new female heads for character creation in Baldur's Gate 3, in one `.pak` file. They are
offered to female Human, Elf, Drow, Half-Elf and Half-Drow characters with the standard body. No Script Extender
and no other mod is needed. Every head is built on the game's own female head, so the skin colour, make-up,
eyebrow and eye textures and the animation are the game's own. Ursula is new in 1.2.0. Wendy also has her own normal
map (the texture that shapes how light falls on the skin); the other 15 heads, Ursula included, use the game's.

## What this is

One file, `SerpentinesCustomHeads.pak`, adds 16 heads to the Head list in character creation. Nothing in the
game is replaced, and no existing character is changed. Each head has its own thumbnail in the head list.

## Who can use them

- **Female Human, Elf, Drow and Half-Elf characters with the standard body.** Each head has one head-list entry
  for each of these four races, 64 entries in all.
- **Half-Drow characters too:** in the game Half-Drow is a kind of Half-Elf, so it uses the Half-Elf heads and has
  no entries of its own.
- Elf and Drow characters get elf ears, Half-Elf and Half-Drow characters half-elf ears, Human characters human
  ears.
- **Not included:** heads for male characters, for the strong body, or for Tiefling, Githyanki, Dwarf, Halfling,
  Gnome, Dragonborn or Half-Orc characters.

## What it does not add

- No skin colour, make-up, tattoo, scar or eyebrow textures: these are the game's own. The one texture of its own
  besides the thumbnails is Wendy's normal map, which gives her skin its relief; the other 15 heads use the game's.
- No new eyeballs, teeth, lashes or hair. These are the game's own parts.
- No change to any existing head, character, companion or NPC.

## About the thumbnails

The thumbnails are pictures of the heads themselves. Each head's thumbnail is tinted with one of the game's own
Human skin colours, from pale to light brown, so that the tiles are easier to tell apart. The tint is on the
thumbnail only: the head's skin colour in the game is not changed.

## Requirements

- **Baldur's Gate 3 on PC.** Tested on Baldur's Gate 3 version 4.1.1.7631656.
- **No Script Extender.** The file contains no scripts and lists no required mod.
- **BG3 Mod Manager** is the way the author installs and tested the mod (see `INSTALL.md`).

## Install

Short version: close the game, import the `.pak` into BG3 Mod Manager (or copy it into the game's `Mods`
folder), move "Serpentine's Custom Heads" to the active list, then **Save** and **Export** the load order. No
load-order position is needed. Full steps, and how to update from an earlier copy: `INSTALL.md`.

## Choosing a head

- **New character:** in character creation, pick a female Human, Elf, Drow, Half-Elf or Half-Drow with the
  standard body and open the Head list. The 16 heads are listed by the names above.
- **Your own custom character later in the game:** the Magic Mirror in camp lists the same 16 heads.
- **Origin companions:** the game's Magic Mirror does not change them.

## Compatibility

- **Other head mods:** these heads are extra entries beside theirs. Every file of this mod is in its own
  folders, and it replaces no game file.

## Known limitations

- **Dolores 1 and Dolores 2:** a small mark can show just past the corner of the mouth, on the viewer's left.

## Removing the mod

1. **First give every character that uses one of these heads another head, and save.**
2. Close the game. In BG3 Mod Manager move "Serpentine's Custom Heads" to the inactive list, then **Save** and
   **Export** the load order.
3. Close BG3 Mod Manager, and only then delete `SerpentinesCustomHeads.pak` from the `Mods` folder.

Do it in this order. If the load order still lists a mod whose file is gone, the game can drop your whole mod
list. Details: `INSTALL.md`.

## AI use disclosure

AI was used in the creation of these head models, in a user-guided process where the user selected reference images and guided conversion of those images into usable 3D meshes in Blender, which were then converted into BG3 compatible models, with human quality control and human directed modifications.

Limited AI generated textures have been used to complete a limited number of the head mods.

## FAQ

**Do I need anything else?**
No. One file, no Script Extender, no other mod.

**Why are there two Altariels, two Dolores and two Undomiels?**
They are two variants of one face. Each variant is its own entry in the head list; use whichever you like.

**Does it run scripts?**
No.
