# Changelog - Serpentine's Custom Heads

**TL/DR:** 1.2.0 adds a sixteenth head, Ursula; the 15 heads of 1.1.0 are unchanged. 1.1.0 added Wendy, with her own
normal map. 1.0.0 was the first public release: 14 female heads in one `.pak` file.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-07

Same file name and same mod as 1.0.0 and 1.1.0 (`SerpentinesCustomHeads.pak`), module version 1.2.0.0. It replaces
1.0.0 and 1.1.0.

- Added **Ursula**, the sixteenth head (female Human, Elf, Drow and Half-Elf; Half-Drow uses the Half-Elf entries;
  standard body). She uses the game's normal map, like every head except Wendy.
- Now 16 heads, 64 head-list entries and 48 thumbnails. Ursula's thumbnails are tinted with one of the game's Human
  skin colours that no other head in the mod uses.
- The 15 heads of 1.1.0 and their head-list entries are unchanged; their thumbnails show the same pictures.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-02

Same file name and same mod as 1.0.0 (`SerpentinesCustomHeads.pak`), module version 1.1.0.0. It replaces 1.0.0.

- Added **Wendy**, the fifteenth head (female Human, Elf, Drow and Half-Elf; Half-Drow uses the Half-Elf entries;
  standard body).
- Wendy has her own normal map (the texture that shapes how light falls on the skin). Her skin colour, make-up and
  eyebrows are the game's own, as for the other heads.
- Now 15 heads, 60 head-list entries and 45 thumbnails. Wendy's thumbnails are tinted with one of the game's Human
  skin colours that no other head in the mod uses.
- The 14 heads of 1.0.0 and their head-list entries are unchanged; their thumbnails show the same pictures in the
  same places.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-24

First public release. One file, `SerpentinesCustomHeads.pak`, module version 1.0.0.0.

Heads (listed alphabetically): Altariel 1, Altariel 2, Ayrin, Debbie, Deborah, Dolores 1, Dolores 2, Ellen,
Juliette, Margot, Ripley, Samantha, Undomiel 1, Undomiel 2.

- Each head: female Human, Elf, Drow and Half-Elf (Half-Drow uses the Half-Elf entries); standard body; 56
  head-list entries in all.
- 42 thumbnails (for each head one with human, one with elf and one with half-elf ears; Drow uses the elf one),
  pictures of the heads themselves. Each head's thumbnails are tinted with one of the game's own Human skin
  colours; the heads' own skin colour in the game is unchanged.
- Known limitations: `README.md`.
